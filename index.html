Why use this extension?

Stop wasting time on jobs that don't match your profile. Let our smart algorithms do the heavy lifting.

Smart Scoring

Instantly see how well you fit a role with our 0-100% match score, based on cosine similarity and keyword extraction.

Advanced Filtering

Filter by graduation date, school year, and multiple industries at once. Never apply to an ineligible job again.

Privacy First

Your resume and data never leave your browser. All processing happens locally using pdf.js.

Bulk Actions

Save all highly-matched jobs to your favorites with a single click. Streamline your workflow.

PDF Parsing

Simply upload your PDF resume. We extract the text and skills automatically to find your best matches.

Seamless Integration

Works directly on top of the NUWorks (Symplicity) website. No new tabs, no context switching.
