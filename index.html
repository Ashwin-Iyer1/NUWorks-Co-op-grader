Held-out evaluation · 820 pairs

Better at putting the right jobs first

The new int8 BGE model improves both rank correlation and top-of-list quality over the previous shipped model. Spearman measures how consistently stronger matches rank above weaker ones; NDCG@10 rewards putting the best jobs near the top.

Spearman correlation 0.666 from 0.515 with the previous model 29% higher rank correlation
NDCG@10 0.848 from 0.787 with the previous model 67.4% top-choice accuracy

Int8 ONNX, semantic-only ranking on an untouched, resume-grouped test split (46 rankable resumes). Top-choice accuracy was 58.7% previously. On the separate 962-pair NeuralFrame test, Spearman was 0.611. Public-dataset metrics are ranking measures, not hiring success rates.

01

Keyword layer

The classic scorer checks required skills, keywords, and eligibility — fast, transparent, and always on. It now also credits implied skills: React on your resume counts toward a JavaScript requirement.

02

Semantic layer

mdbr-leaf-mt-resume-grader is now a 33.36M-parameter BGE-small fine-tune trained on 26,677 resume–job pairs. It embeds your resume and each posting, then measures how close they sit in meaning. “Built REST services in Flask” counts toward “backend web development”.

03

Blended score

Your final match is 65% keyword + 35% semantic. Every card shows the split (Keyword 96 · AI 79) and the details view adds a dedicated Semantic (AI) bar so you can see exactly what moved the number.

Explainable, down to the bar

Open any posting and the match breakdown shows Skills, Keywords, and Semantic (AI) side by side. Hover a card's split to see the raw cosine similarity behind the AI number.

  • Private by design. Your resume and every job description are embedded in a Web Worker in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Lightweight until you opt in. The extension ships without the model. Ticking “Semantic AI” downloads the runtime and model once (~57 MB) and keeps them cached.
  • Scores never wait on it. Keyword scores appear instantly; the AI refinement streams in behind a “Refining scores” chip and never blocks the page.
  • Honest about hardware. Before enabling, you're told it's compute-intensive on MacBook Airs, Chromebooks and older laptops. Thread count auto-scales to your CPU.
