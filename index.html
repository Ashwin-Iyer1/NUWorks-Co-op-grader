Why use this extension?

Stop wasting time on jobs that don't match your profile. Let our smart algorithms do the heavy lifting.

NLP Powered Qualification

Uses a trained Natural Language Processing model to detect subtle requirements like graduation dates and school years automatically.

Smart Match Scoring

Instantly see how well you fit a role with our 0-100% match score, based on cosine similarity and keyword extraction.

Smart Filters

Filter jobs by match score, freshness (days posted), and application status. Hide ineligible jobs automatically.

Resume Parsing

Simply upload your PDF resume. We extract the text and skills automatically to find your best matches.

Persistent State

Remembers your search results, last active view, and filter settings so you find opportunities faster.

Privacy First

Your resume and data never leave your browser. All processing happens locally within the extension.
