01 — Features

Built for how students actually search for co-ops.

NUWorks is powerful but noisy. This extension adds a scoring layer so you spend time on postings that match your background — not scrolling through hundreds.

📄

Resume-Aware Matching

Upload your resume once. The extension extracts skills, frameworks, and tools to score each job against your actual experience.

🔒

Privacy by Default

All NLP processing runs in your browser. Your resume, scores, and browsing data never touch a server.

⚡

Batch Analysis

Analyze up to 200 postings in one pass. Filter by score, job type, and qualification level to surface the best matches instantly.

📊

Score Breakdown

See exactly which skills matched and which are missing. Understand where you stand before writing a cover letter.

🏷️

Smart Filters

Filter by company, title keyword, score range, or recency. Hide jobs you've already applied to or that require qualifications you don't have.

💾

Export Results

Save your filtered results as structured data. Review them later or share with your co-op advisor.

02 — How It Works

Three steps. No account required.

01

Install the extension

Add it from the Chrome Web Store. It requests only the permissions it needs to read NUWorks pages.

→
02

Upload your resume

Paste text or upload a PDF. The extension parses it locally and stores your skill profile in browser storage.

→
03

Open NUWorks and analyze

Navigate to the job listings page, click "Analyze Jobs," and let the extension score every visible posting.
