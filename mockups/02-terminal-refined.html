Features

What the extension does.

Six tools to cut through NUWorks noise and surface the postings worth your time.

01
Resume Matching
Upload your resume once. Skills, tools, and frameworks are extracted locally and used to score every posting.
02
Local Processing
All NLP runs in-browser. No API calls, no tracking, no data exfiltration. Your resume stays on your machine.
03
Batch Analysis
Analyze up to 200 job postings at once. Results update in real time as each posting is processed.
04
Skill Breakdown
Each score shows matched and missing skills so you know exactly where you stand before applying.
05
Advanced Filters
Filter by title, company, score range, recency, and eligibility. Hide jobs you've already applied to.
06
Export
Save filtered results as structured data for later review or to share with your co-op advisor.
Setup

Running in under a minute.

No account creation. No configuration files. Three steps and you're scoring postings.

Install

Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store. It only requests permissions to read NUWorks pages.

Add your resume

Paste text or upload a PDF. Skill extraction happens locally in milliseconds.

Analyze

Navigate to NUWorks job listings, click the extension, and hit "Analyze Jobs."
