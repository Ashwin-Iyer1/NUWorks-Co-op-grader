Features

Tools that make co-op search practical.

NUWorks has hundreds of postings. These features help you find the ones worth reading.

📄

Resume Matching

Upload once. The extension extracts your skills and scores every posting against them.

🔒

Private by Design

NLP runs in-browser. No servers, no tracking, no API calls. Your resume stays local.

📊

Skill Breakdown

See exactly which skills matched and which are missing for every job.

⚡

Batch Analysis

Score up to 200 postings at once with real-time progress feedback.

🔍

Advanced Filters

Filter by title, company, score range, job type, and eligibility.

💾

Export Results

Save filtered results for later review or to share with your advisor.

Getting Started

Set up in under a minute.

No account needed. No configuration. Just install, upload, and analyze.

1

Install the extension

Add from the Chrome Web Store. Only NUWorks page permissions are requested.

2

Upload your resume

Paste text or upload a PDF. Skills are extracted locally in milliseconds.

3

Analyze postings

Open NUWorks, click the extension, and hit Analyze. Results appear in real time.
