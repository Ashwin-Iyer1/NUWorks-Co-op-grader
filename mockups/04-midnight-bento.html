Features

Built for how students actually search for co-ops.

NUWorks is powerful but noisy. This extension cuts through it.

🧠

NLP-Powered Qualification Detection

Uses a trained model to detect subtle requirements like graduation dates, school year restrictions, and eligibility criteria that manual scanning would miss.

📄

Resume Parsing

Upload a PDF. Skills, tools, and frameworks are extracted locally and used to score every posting.

🏷️

Visual Badges

See match scores, external warnings, and eligibility with color-coded badges directly on the job list.

🔍

Smart Filters & Batch Actions

Filter by match score, freshness, company, title, or eligibility. Save all matched jobs in one click. Hide applied or ineligible postings to clean up your list.

🔒

Privacy First

All processing runs in your browser. Your resume and data never leave your machine. Zero server calls.

⚙️

Manual Control

Toggle auto-grading, trigger analysis manually, update settings. You decide when and how it runs.

Setup

Running in under a minute.

01

Install the extension

Add it from the Chrome Web Store. It only requests permissions to read NUWorks pages.

02

Upload your resume

Open the extension on NUWorks and upload your resume PDF. Skills are extracted locally.

03

Analyze jobs

Click "Analyze Jobs" to see your match scores and filter results instantly.
