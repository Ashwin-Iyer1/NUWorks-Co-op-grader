Why use this extension?

Stop scrolling through hundreds of postings. Let the extension surface the jobs that actually match your background.

01 NLP Qualification Detection Trained model detects graduation dates, school year restrictions, and eligibility criteria automatically.
02 Auto-Grading & Badges Match scores, external application warnings, and eligibility status shown as color-coded badges on the job list.
03 Smart Filters Filter by match score, freshness, company, title, or eligibility. Save matched jobs in one click.
04 Resume Parsing Upload a PDF resume. Skills and tools extracted locally and matched against every posting.
05 Manual Control Toggle auto-grading, trigger analysis manually, update profile settings at any time.
06 Privacy First All processing runs in your browser. Your resume, scores, and data never touch a server.
