Features

Cultivated for the Northeastern co-op grind

Spend your hours on the postings that actually fit — not scrolling past the ones that don't.

🧠

NLP qualification

A trained model reads each posting for graduation dates, class-year limits, and hidden eligibility rules.

🏷️

Auto-grade & badges

Color-coded match scores and warnings appear inline on the job list the moment a page loads.

🔍

Smart filters

Filter by score, freshness, company, or title. Save every strong match with a single click.

📄

Résumé parsing

Drop in a PDF. Skills are extracted locally and matched against every posting on the board.

⚙️

Manual control

Toggle auto-grading, re-run analysis on demand, and tune your profile whenever you like.

🔒

Privacy first

Your résumé and data never leave the browser. There are no servers and no accounts.

Getting started

Planted in under a minute

No account. No configuration. Install, upload, analyze.

1

Install

Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store. Only NUWorks page permissions are needed.

2

Upload

Drop in your résumé PDF. Skills are extracted locally in milliseconds.

3

Analyze

Open NUWorks and hit Analyze. Match scores and badges appear in real time.
