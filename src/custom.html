Enable Semantic AI matching?

A small AI language model (mdbr-leaf-ir, 23M parameters) runs entirely on your device and re-reads every job description to score how well it fits your resume by meaning, not just keyword overlap — so "built REST services in Flask" counts toward "backend web development experience". Match scores become a blend of 65% keyword and 35% semantic similarity. Your resume and the job text never leave your computer.

Enabling downloads the AI runtime and model (~46 MB, one time); they are kept for future sessions.

Heads up: scoring is computationally intensive. On lower-powered machines (MacBook Air, Chromebooks, older laptops) a large job batch can take several minutes in the background and may spin up fans or drain battery. Scores stay usable the whole time — the AI refinement just arrives later.
